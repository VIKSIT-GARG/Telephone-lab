# Candidate Hindi Pitfalls / preservation fixtures

76 assistant-authored engineering examples, dated 31 October 2026. Authorship and review status are recorded per case. None has been reviewed by a human or native speaker. They are NOT eligible for headline benchmark accuracy. Expected labels are hypotheses for regression tests, not certified linguistic ground truth.

Cases cover time/date ambiguity, Hindi fractions/number words, cross-script contacts, qualifiers, polarity, conditions, certainty, places, order and added claims. The source and retelling are synthetic and fictional. No personal contact data is intentionally included.

The checker and these fixtures were developed together; performance on them is subject to severe test-set bias. They do not estimate translation-provider quality. Use exact verdicts and uncertainty flags separately from mutation-detection recall. Human review must check wording, expected label, context and script accuracy; record reviewer ID/date and retain previous versions. Do not mark a case reviewed simply because tests pass.
